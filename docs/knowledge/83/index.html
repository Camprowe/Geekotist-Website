Batman: Arkham City Source

Zsasz Wasn't the Only One Left on the Cutting Room Floor

Looking back across the series, Zsasz's expanded role kept getting trimmed further in later games too, with unused audio files turning up online of him interacting with Batman in scenes that never made the final cut. It shows Rocksteady clearly liked the character a lot more than what actually reached players.

Batman: Arkham Asylum Source

Conroy, Hamill and Sorkin Reunite for the Game

Rocksteady didn't just cast any actors for Batman, Joker and Harley - they brought back Kevin Conroy, Mark Hamill and Arleen Sorkin from Batman: The Animated Series. For a lot of fans that alone made the game feel like the definitive take on these characters, since it was basically the animated show's cast stepping into a new medium.

Grand Theft Auto IV Source

The Niko That Almost Wasn't

Niko's look was partly based on actor Vladimir Mashkov, who's known for playing Sasha in Behind Enemy Lines, and Rockstar actually approached him to voice the character. He turned it down - reportedly he wasn't that interested in video games, Rockstar were being extremely tight-lipped about the whole project, and the recording setup would've meant constant flights between Moscow and New York. Word is he ended up regretting the decision once the game blew up.

Fallout (TV Series) Source

The Vault Cameras Cut To Static Mid-Massacre

When raiders storm Vault 33 and things turn violent, the internal broadcast system that's been playing that cheery propaganda footage actually glitches out and falls back to the 'Please Stand By' card again, this time as the wedding descends into chaos behind it. Nice bit of visual irony from the production team.

Arcane Source

Cupcakes Are a Caitlyn Callback

Right at the start of episode one, when Vi, Mylo, Powder and Claggor are legging it across the rooftops, Claggor grabs a cupcake off a tray mid-heist. Looks like a throwaway gag, but that exact cupcake trap is Caitlyn's in-game ability Yordle Snap Trap, icon and all. Vi even calls Caitlyn 'Cupcake' as a nickname later in the show, so the whole thing loops back around nicely.

Grand Theft Auto IV Source

The Heart of Liberty City

There's a hidden chamber inside the Statue of Happiness that most players never find, and honestly it's not exactly signposted - there's literally a sign at the base that says 'No Hidden Content This Way,' which is Rockstar being cheeky. Steal a helicopter, land on the upper platform of the pedestal, and climb a ladder up through the statue's body. What's waiting for you is a huge, slowly beating heart wrapped in chains. Nobody's ever officially explained what it's supposed to mean, it's just there for the people determined enough to go looking.

Fallout (TV Series) Source

Goggins Avoided The Games On Purpose

Rather than boning up on the source material, Walton Goggins deliberately steered clear of playing the Fallout games before stepping into the role, choosing instead to build the Ghoul's persona from classic Western films, drawing on the kind of weathered gunslinger energy you'd get from John Wayne or Clint Eastwood.

Arcane Source

Powder's Sketchbook Hides a Skin Reference

In episode three, while Powder's sat crying next to her drawings, you can spot a crude sketch of a pair of pink-outlined wings among the doodles of Fishbones and dynamite. That's a nod to Star Guardian Jinx, one of her flashier in-game skins, tucked into the show years before most viewers would've clocked it.

Halo 3: ODST Source

Number Six Is Running Your Squad

Veronica Dare, the ONI officer who takes command of the squad before the drop, is voiced by Tricia Helfer. Sci-fi fans will know her better as Number Six from the rebooted Battlestar Galactica, so ODST quietly doubles as a bit of a genre casting crossover.

The Super Mario Bros. Movie Source

Foreman Spike's Grudge

Mario and Luigi's old boss Spike isn't just a random side character, he's pulled straight from Wrecking Crew, a fairly obscure 1985 Famicom title. In the movie he gets a dig in at Mario, calling him a joke, clearly still bitter about being his old employer.
